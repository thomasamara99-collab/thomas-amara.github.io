Most savings apps make you feel guilty. Aside does the opposite — log the purchases you resist, and watch that money push a goal you actually care about forward. No shame, no streaks to break, no leaderboards. Just proof you're getting better at the one skill that actually moves the needle.

Why Aside

Five things that make the resist-log loop actually stick.

Say no, watch it count

Tempted by something you don't need? Log it in seconds. Pip, Aside's resident squirrel, cheers every single time — and the money you resisted spending moves straight toward a goal you actually chose.

Make more, it counts too

Closing the gap on a goal isn't only about spending less — it's about making more. Log extra income toward a goal exactly like you log a resisted purchase. Both are progress. Both get celebrated.

See yourself get better

A weekly completion ring you close, never a streak you fear breaking. A real trend of your resisted spending over time, compared only to your own last 30 days — never to anyone else.

Build goals that feel real

Create funds for a trip, an emergency cushion, a big purchase — anything. Set a target date and get a calm, once-and-done "suggested weekly amount," never a nagging notification.

Meet Pip, your cheerleader

A warm companion that celebrates restraint and cheers milestones — never once makes you feel bad about a "yes." The anti-guilt-trip mascot for the anti-guilt-trip app.

Built to be honest, not gamified

Aside never links to your bank — everything is entered by you, on your terms. No scraping, no sharing, no surprises. Free covers your first 5 funds and the full resist-log loop; Premium adds unlimited funds, full resisted-purchase history and insights, gentle reminders, and CSV export.
