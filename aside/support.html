Common questions

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Aside Premium is billed through the Apple App Store, so subscriptions are managed there, not in the app. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Aside. Apple handles renewals, cancellations, and refund requests directly.

I resubscribed or got a new phone — how do I get Premium back?

Open Aside → Settings → Restore purchases. This reconnects your existing subscription to your account without charging you again.

How do I export my data?

Premium includes a full CSV export: Settings → Export data. This gives you every fund, resisted purchase, expense, and income entry you've logged.

How do I delete my account and data?

Settings → Delete account, inside the app. This permanently removes your account and everything associated with it from Aside's database — it can't be undone, so export first if you want to keep a copy.

Does Aside link to my bank?

No. Aside never connects to your bank or card. Every figure in the app is one you've entered yourself, on purpose.

My data isn't syncing across devices

Syncing requires an account (email or Sign in with Apple) — in local/guest mode, data stays on that one device only. If you're signed in on both devices and still don't see your data, email me and I'll help track it down.

Something crashed / looks wrong

Email me what happened, ideally with the screen you were on and what you tapped — that's usually enough for me to reproduce and fix it.
